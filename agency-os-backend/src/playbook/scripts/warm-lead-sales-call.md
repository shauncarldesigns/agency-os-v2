## Stage: opening

“Hey [Name], it’s Shaun with Shaun Carl Designs. I’m the one who sent over the website I put together for [Company Name]. Did I catch you at an okay time?”

“Were you able to take a look at it?”

“Great. What stood out to you?”

“What would you want changed or added if it were actually going to become your website?”

“Absolutely. What I sent was really just a starting point based on what I could find online. We would customize all of that around your business.”

> Let them talk about the design, photos, services, colors, wording, or anything that feels inaccurate.

## Stage: needs

“The bigger question is what you need from it right now.”

“Are you mainly looking for a professional website so people have somewhere credible to go when they look you up, or are you actively trying to get found by more people and bring in more clients?”

> Listen for professional presence versus active growth. Use the plan buttons in the call context to follow their answer.

## Stage: presence

“That sounds like the Build & Maintain plan.”

“We would take what you have already seen, customize it around your business, get everything approved, and launch it under your domain.”

“The website is $799, and then hosting and ongoing management are $79 per month. The hosting drops to $50 per month with a one-year commitment.”

“Does that sound like what you’re looking for?”

> Pause for agreement. The closing stage handles paperwork and discovery, so do not explain those steps here.

## Stage: growth

“That sounds more like the Growth plan.”

“The website gives people a professional place to land, but a website by itself does not automatically bring in new clients.”

“The Growth plan includes the website, hosting, and the ongoing Google Business and local-search work designed to improve your visibility and help more people find you.”

“It’s $499 per month.”

“With a six-month commitment, the website build is reduced from $799 to $499. With a twelve-month commitment, the website build is waived.”

“Does the six-month or twelve-month option make more sense for where you are right now?”

## Stage: unsure

“Do you feel like you already have enough work and mainly need the business to look more professional online?”

“Or would you like us actively working each month to improve your visibility and help bring in additional opportunities?”

> Recommend the plan based on their answer, then select it in the call context.

## Stage: next-steps

“Great. I’ll send the agreement and initial deposit invoice after this call.”

“Before we wrap up, let’s reserve a discovery time so the project can keep moving once those are completed.”

> This stage confirms the administrative next step. The next screen handles only scheduling and discovery.

## Stage: discovery

“What does your schedule look like over the next few days?”

“The discovery call is where we turn the concept you saw into your actual website. We’ll go through what you want changed, the services and areas you want emphasized, your business story, photos, branding, frequently asked questions, and where calls and form submissions should go.”

“Perfect. I have you down for [day and time].”

> Do not leave the discovery call unscheduled. Choose a specific date and time.

## Stage: website-process

“After discovery, I’ll update the site using the information we collected, replace the placeholder content, and add your real photos and branding.”

“I’ll send the completed version to you for review, and you’ll be able to request changes before it goes live.”

“Once you approve it, you’ll purchase or provide access to the domain. You will own the domain, and I’ll handle connecting it and setting up the technical side.”

“After the domain is connected and the final payment is completed, the website goes live.”

## Stage: growth-process

“Once the website is live, the ongoing Growth work begins.”

“That includes the Google Business Profile and local-search work we discussed. The website becomes the foundation, and then we continue improving your visibility each month.”

> Use this stage only for a Growth client.

## Stage: full-close

“Perfect. Watch for the agreement and invoice from me today. Once those are completed, we’ll be ready to get started.”

> Confirm the selected plan and discovery time in your notes, record the call, and end here without repeating the offer or reopening the sale.
